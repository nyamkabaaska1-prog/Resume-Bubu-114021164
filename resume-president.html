Dr. Tsai Ing-wen

President of the Republic of China (Taiwan)

Office of the President, No. 122, Section 1, Chongqing S. Rd., Zhongzheng District, Taipei City 10048, Taiwan (R.O.C.)

Profile

An experienced leader and stateswoman serving as the President of the Republic of China (Taiwan). The first woman to be elected to the office. Known for a pragmatic and steady approach to governance, with a focus on national defense, indigenous rights, and social welfare.

Education

  • Doctor of Law (LL.D.), London School of Economics and Political Science (LSE), University of London, 1984
  • Master of Laws (LL.M.), Cornell University Law School, 1980
  • Bachelor of Laws (LL.B.), National Taiwan University, 1978

Professional & Political Experience

  • 2020-Present: President of the Republic of China (Taiwan) - Second Term
  • 2016-2020: President of the Republic of China (Taiwan) - First Term
  • 2014-2015: Chairperson, Democratic Progressive Party (DPP)
  • 2010-2012: Chairperson, Democratic Progressive Party (DPP)
  • 2006-2007: Vice Premier, Executive Yuan
  • 2004-2006: Member, Legislative Yuan (At-large Representative)
  • 2000-2004: Chairperson, Mainland Affairs Council

Key Achievements & Policies

  • Led the nation's successful response to the COVID-19 pandemic, earning international recognition.
  • Oversaw the implementation of national defense reforms and increased domestic defense production.
  • Championed the "5+2" Industrial Innovation Plan to transform Taiwan's industrial base.
  • Presided over a period of significant economic growth and stability.
  • Issued a historic formal apology to the nation's Indigenous peoples on behalf of the government.
  • Advanced social welfare policies, including long-term care 2.0 and pension reform.

Academic Experience

  • Professor, Soochow University School of Law
  • Professor, National Chengchi University
  • Associate Professor, National Taiwan University
